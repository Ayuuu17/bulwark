Questions

Straight answers.

Can I scan any website?

No. You can only scan domains you prove you control, with a DNS record or a file on the site. Scanning systems you don't own or have permission to test is against our Acceptable Use Policy and may be illegal.

Will a scan break or slow down my site?

No. Website scans make a handful of normal requests, like one visitor opening your homepage. We never try to log in, guess hidden pages or send attack payloads.

Do you keep my code?

No. Uploads are analyzed in memory and discarded when the scan finishes. Your report keeps short snippets (single lines) so you can find each issue, with secrets redacted.

Who sees my code if I turn on AI review?

The most security-relevant files are sent to our AI provider to be reviewed, under that provider's data terms. You can switch AI review off for any scan. See the Privacy Policy.

Does this replace a penetration test?

No. Automated scanning catches common, well-known problems quickly and cheaply. A manual test by a qualified professional can find issues automation can't.
